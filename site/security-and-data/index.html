MeTaBos security and data boundaries

MeTaBos is designed around explicit approvals, local durable records and inspectable evidence. Its zero-trust name is a design goal, not an independent certification or a claim of complete production security.

Does all data stay on my device?

Local business records persist on the configured host. Browser WebLLM inference runs on the capable browser after an explicit model download. Cloud model calls send approved prompts to the configured provider, whose own retention terms apply. Prompts and outputs in the persistent task workspace are durable operator records. There is no blanket zero-retention guarantee.

What does the audit chain prove?

The application can verify a local hash chain and detect inconsistencies relative to its current records. A database administrator can rewrite an entire unanchored chain. Hashes do not establish an external timestamp, a passkey signature or a zero-knowledge proof of model computation.

What protects the workspace?

The startup wrapper restricts network binding before operator setup. Sessions, origin checks, transaction policies and payload-bound approvals protect specific operations. Public Meta webhooks use their own signature checks and durable intake. Crawling directives are privacy hygiene, not access control.

What remains to qualify?

Individual accounts/RBAC, passkeys, multi-tenant isolation, production recovery, retention/deletion policy, isolated tool execution and independent security review remain separate gates. Provider-dependent workspaces do not remove those requirements. Use private TLS and current dependency/security updates before a pilot.
