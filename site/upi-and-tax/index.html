UPI requests, settlement evidence and tax in MuzeBOS

A UPI payment URI requests payment. It does not prove settlement. MuzeBOS keeps pending invoices distinct from operator-recorded reconciliation and any future provider-verified evidence.

How does payment reconciliation work?

The merchant independently checks the bank or PSP record before recording a reference. The UI labels manual reconciliation separately from provider-verified settlement. An email, QR code, generated link or matching amount alone does not establish trusted settlement. No universal zero-fee or fraud-proof UPI claim is made.

Is MuzeBOS a GST filing service?

No. Local invoice calculations use merchant-supplied rates and details. They do not establish GST registration, correct HSN classification, current tax-law compliance, filing or government e-invoice registration. Tax-related modules have bounded local scopes and must be reviewed before use.

Are Stripe, Wise, PayPal and global rails live?

Provider-dependent pages can store configuration references, drafts and queued outbox records. A populated page or a successful connection probe does not mean payments or messages were dispatched. Escrow, mandates, payouts and jurisdiction rules need separate provider/legal implementation and live qualification.
