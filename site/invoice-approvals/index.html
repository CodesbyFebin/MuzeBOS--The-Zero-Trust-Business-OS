How MuzeBOS verifies invoice approval

MuzeBOS binds merchant approval to the exact canonical invoice payload. Issuance rechecks the approved payload and available stock, then writes the issued invoice, inventory movements and audit evidence in one database transaction. An altered or replayed approval cannot authorize a different invoice.

The operator workflow

  1. Add the merchant profile, customer and catalog products.
  2. Create an order draft and review quantities, prices, tax and payload hash.
  3. Explicitly approve the reviewed payload within its expiry window.
  4. Issue the invoice; the transaction rechecks stock and commits durable records.
  5. Inspect the invoice snapshot, stock movement and evidence entry.

Integer minor units avoid floating-point money calculations. Tax uses explicit per-line rounding. Business details and rates remain merchant supplied; exact hash binding does not validate a legal tax interpretation or customer identity.

What happens if another order consumes the stock?

The issuance transaction checks stock again. A stale draft cannot bypass that check merely because it was approved earlier. Idempotency keys identify retries; the same key cannot authorize a changed request. Rejected operations do not partially issue an invoice.

Does AI approval issue an invoice?

No. Model outputs are proposals. The persistent inference workspace has its own exact-task Jev gate and bounded workers. Business writes retain their separate validation, permission and transaction boundaries. A model response cannot grant itself authority.
