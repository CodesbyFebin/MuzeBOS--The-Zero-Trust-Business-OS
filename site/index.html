MuzeBOS documentation

MuzeBOS is a self-hostable business workspace for founders and small businesses. It combines SQLite-backed commerce, customer records, inventory, approvals, audit evidence and experimental AI assistance. CodesbyFebin maintains the MuzeBOS changes on the attributed FounderOS/GladOS source.

Release posture: single-operator preview. Available pages describe bounded local capabilities; provider-dependent pages do not establish successful external transactions. The project name is a design goal, not a security certification. No ranking or production-readiness score is assigned.
